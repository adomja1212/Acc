Weapon models in this folder
============================

All five come from the "Operation Steel Tide" project by AetherRadar
(https://github.com/AetherRadar/operation-steel-tide), whose own additions are MIT
licensed (notice below). For this game they were further modified: spare and loose
magazines, suppressor and optic mounts removed, the AK simplified to fewer
triangles, textures shrunk to 512 px WebP, and the M24's green stock repainted as wood.

- akm.glb    - AK-47 by taradavies (https://opengameart.org/content/ak-47-1), CC0 1.0.
               Textures and adaptation by Operation Steel Tide (MIT).
- scar.glb   - "ScarL" by AdamKokrito (https://poly.pizza/m/ab1V8RlPDc),
               licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
               Modified by Operation Steel Tide and again for this game.
- sniper.glb - AWM from the Quaternius Ultimate Guns Pack, CC0 1.0 (adapted by Operation Steel Tide, MIT).
- kar.glb    - M24 from the Quaternius Ultimate Guns Pack, CC0 1.0 (adapted by Operation Steel Tide, MIT),
               shown in the game as a Kar98k-style bolt-action rifle.
- pistol.glb - P226 from the Quaternius Ultimate Guns Pack, CC0 1.0 (adapted by Operation Steel Tide, MIT).
